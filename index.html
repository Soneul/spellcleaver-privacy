Privacy Policy — Spellcleaver

Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short: Spellcleaver has no account system and no servers of our own. Your game progress is saved on your device. Two Google services are used: Google AdMob, which supplies optional rewarded ads, and Google Play Games, which you can choose to sign in to for cloud saves, a leaderboard and achievements.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no login, no analytics of our own and no servers. The game asks you for a player name, but that name is shown only in the game and is never sent anywhere. We never receive your name, your email address, or any other personal information.

Advertising

Spellcleaver shows ads through Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google receives your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), and general device and app information in order to select, deliver and measure the ad and to prevent fraud. This data goes to Google, not to us. Google's handling of it is governed by its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

The game shows only rewarded ads, and they are always optional. An ad plays only when you tap a button offering something in return (for example a Forge Blessing, a temporary damage boost, a free daily summon, a faster expedition, or doubled gold from time away). There are no ads that play automatically, and nothing in the game can be bought with real money.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device. Doing so does not affect your game progress.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google shares your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) with the game, and the game uses Google's services to:

  • store a copy of your progress in your Google account (Saved Games), so you can continue on another phone
  • submit your highest floor to the game's leaderboard
  • record the achievements you unlock

This data is stored by Google, not by us, under the Google Privacy Policy. You can sign out, change who sees your Play Games activity, or delete the game's Play Games data at any time from your Play Games profile or your Google Account. If you never sign in, none of this happens.

Consent, if you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland

The first time you open the game, Google's User Messaging Platform will ask for your consent choices regarding personalised advertising, and your answer is respected on every subsequent ad request.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress in your device's local app storage:

  • The player name you chose
  • Floor reached, gold, starlight and Embers
  • Companions owned, their levels and ascensions, and your squad
  • Blades in the forge and the best blade forged
  • Expeditions in progress, records and tutorial progress

This never leaves your device. It is included in Android's system backup if you have that enabled, in which case Google handles it under your own Google account settings, not ours. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Academy → Records → Delete all save data in the game deletes it immediately. A cloud copy in your Google account (if you signed in to Google Play Games) can be deleted from your Play Games profile.

Children

Spellcleaver is not directed at children under 13. It contains no chat, no user-generated content and no social features.

Your rights

Because no personal data reaches us, there is no account with us to access, correct, export or delete. The data the game holds is on your device and under your control: clear it from the game, from Android's app storage settings, or by uninstalling. For the advertising identifier, reset or delete it in Android's own Settings → Privacy → Ads, and use the consent options described above to control personalised advertising.

Changes

If a future version changes what is collected, this policy will be updated before that version is released, the change will be noted in the release notes, and the Play Store Data safety section will be updated to match.
